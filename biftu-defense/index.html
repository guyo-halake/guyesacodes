BIFTU ORBIT

Autonomous Swarm Management & Defensive Fleet Control

Biftu Orbit is an integrated software and hardware system engineered to orchestrate large fleets of autonomous uncrewed vehicles across land, air, and coastal waterways. Orbit coordinates drone patrols, border inspection UGVs, and emergency search units without requiring constant 1-to-1 joystick control.

  • Fleet Swarm Coordination: Single operator supervises dozens of autonomous patrol drones and ground rovers simultaneously.
  • Anti-Jamming Mesh Telemetry: High-bandwidth distributed radio mesh that self-heals dynamically if individual nodes are jammed or damaged.
  • Strict Human Oversight: Enforces human-in-the-loop verification protocols for any actionable or defensive countermeasures.
