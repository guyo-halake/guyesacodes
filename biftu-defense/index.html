SYSTEM 02 // AUTONOMOUS RECONNAISSANCE

Orbit UAV

Long-Endurance Autonomous Airborne Reconnaissance

Orbit is a carbon-composite uncrewed aircraft built for persistent border monitoring and critical energy corridor patrol. High-efficiency solar cells embedded into the wings extend electric flight loiter to 36 continuous hours.

  • Persistent Loiter: Ultra-low wing loading and solar recharging support day-and-night patrol cycles.
  • Self-Healing Mesh Radio: Acts as an airborne communication relay node for ground teams and vehicles.
  • Autonomous Navigation: Operates reliably through GPS-denied environments using optical ground tracking.

SYSTEM 03 // UNMANNED GROUND VEHICLE

Sentinel UGV

Autonomous Ground Vehicle for Perimeter Security

Sentinel is a tracked, all-terrain autonomous ground rover built to patrol remote industrial facilities, oil pipelines, and sensitive sovereign borders. It navigates rugged terrain autonomously, logging anomalies and alerting central command.

  • All-Terrain Mobility: Continuous rubber tracks climb 45-degree inclines, wet mud, and rock beds.
  • 360-Degree Sensing: Solid-state LiDAR, stereo optical cameras, and infrared optics identify perimeter incursions.
  • Failsafe Autonomous Return: Automatic return-to-base and automated inductive charging docking.

SYSTEM 04 // DOMESTIC MANUFACTURING

Indigenous Hardware Foundry

Multi-Layer Electronics Fabrication & Hardened Microcontrollers

Biftu operates domestic electronics manufacturing in Nairobi. By controlling circuit schematic design, multi-layer printed circuit board assembly, and microcontroller firmware internally, systems remain free of untrusted third-party silicon backdoors.

  • Automated SMT Assembly: High-precision pick-and-place robotics with automated optical inspection (AOI).
  • Hardened RTOS Firmware: Deterministic real-time operating code authored in Rust and modern C.
  • Environmental Hardening: Conformal coating protects circuits against tropical humidity, salt fog, and dust.
